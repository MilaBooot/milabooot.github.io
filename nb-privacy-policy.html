01

Who We Are

NewsBrief is a news summarization app that delivers short, clear, 3-sentence summaries of world news — in your language. We do not run ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not require you to create an account.

02

What Data We Collect

NewsBrief stores only what you enter: your display name and your chosen assistant nickname. These are saved locally on your device only — they are never uploaded to our servers.

We do not collect:

  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Location data of any kind
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Reading history or behaviour
  • Any biometric data
03

News Content

Stories are sourced from reputable third-party news providers. Summaries are generated automatically and are provided for informational purposes only. NewsBrief does not endorse or guarantee the accuracy of any summarized content. Always refer to the original source for full context.

04

Voice & Audio

The text-to-speech feature uses your device's built-in speech engine to read stories aloud. No audio is recorded or transmitted. If you grant microphone permission (for future voice commands), audio is processed locally on your device and is never sent to NewsBrief servers.

05

Third-Party Services

NewsBrief may use third-party APIs for news fetching and translation. These services are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not share personally identifiable information with any third-party service. Requests to these services may include your selected language preference.

06

No Tracking, No Analytics

We do not use advertising SDKs, cross-app trackers, analytics platforms, or crash reporting tools that send data off your device. Your reading behaviour — which stories you open, how long you read — stays entirely on your device.

07

Children's Privacy

NewsBrief is designed to be safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because no account is required and no personal data is uploaded, no special parental consent flow is necessary.

08

Intellectual Property

News content remains the intellectual property of its original publishers. Summaries are transformative in nature and link back to the original source. NewsBrief's design, code, and branding are the property of NewsBrief and may not be reproduced without permission.

09

NewsBrief is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind, express or implied. We make no guarantees about the completeness, accuracy, or timeliness of news content. Your use of the app is at your own risk.
